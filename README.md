I got access to computers at quite a young age, which might not exactly be the best thing, but nonetheless it led me to be what I am. A big part of me is in my endless curiosity, and the need for a little chaos.
Cybersecurity always fascinated me since computers host an ever enlarging world that far as I can see has no end in sight and cybersecurity or just understanding computers as a whole has paved a way for me to feed my curiosity, possibly endlessly. The reason I wanna join whitehats is simply because I've a deep rooted interest in computers and anywhere i can learn just a little more about it, preferably with others who also enjoy it is somewhere I'd love to be.

A recent event I heard about was the XZ Utils backdoor (CVE-2024-3094), discovered in March 2024.
I watched a video on it by veritasium while eating (like one does) and was fascinated by the thought process of the attacker. Keeping the morality or legality of it aside, the attack was very well planned, and even executed. The backdoor was even implemented in some bleeding-edged distros such as Fedora Rawhide/40 betas, Debian testing, Kali etc. and was caught by a Microsoft engineer who noticed a 500ms delay in ssh logins."500ms" negligible enough to be ignored, yet huge enough to compromise every single computer running linux.
